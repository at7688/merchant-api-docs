Every merchant request is **HTTPS POST**, **JSON**, **UTF-8**. Inner params are JSON, then **AES-256-GCM** with your 32-byte hex **ApiKey**. Put the hex ciphertext in outer `data`.

| | URL |
|---|---|
| Test | `https://api.at-dev.app/api/v1` |
| Production | `https://api.at-gaming.app/api/v1` |

Whitelist your server egress IPs.

## Two wallet types

**Transfer wallet**: player balance lives on the platform. You call `/player/*` and `/transfer/*`.

**Seamless wallet**: player balance lives with you. You still call the same account/login/record APIs. **Bets and payouts are callbacks to your URL** (`/player/balance`, `/player/bet`, `/player/win`, `/player/bet_refund`). Set that base URL in the back office.

## How to read results

- Success: `code` is **`OK`** (not `"200"`).
- Auth/business errors: usually **HTTP 200** with a non-OK `code`.
- **HTTP 500** = result unknown. Body **may not be JSON**. For transfers, call `/transfer/check`; do not treat 500 as a confirmed failure.

See [Protocol](/en/protocol) and [Error codes](/en/errors).
