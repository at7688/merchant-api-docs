title: Game records
description: Bets, JP, reports, scratch cards.
---

Times are Unix seconds. `end_at` must be greater than `start_at`. `acc` is optional.

`/bet_rec/list`, `/jp_rec/list`, `/bet_report/list`, `/scratch_card_rec/list` allow **10** calls per merchant per minute (`TOO_MANY_REQUESTS`).

## POST `/game/list`

No inner params. `data.list`: `code`, `name`, `game_category_code`, `game_type_code`, `id`.

## POST `/bet_rec/list`

`page` (≥1), `perpage` (1–1000), `start_at`, `end_at`, optional `acc`.

## POST `/bet_rec/detail`

`sn` (required, max 120), `lang` (BCP 47).

## POST `/bet_rec/query`

Status for up to 100 sns (reconciliation).

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `sns` | yes | 1–100 ids |

Each item: `sn`, `status`. `UNKNOWN` means we have no record. **Do not treat UNKNOWN as “debit never happened” and refund on your side.** Contact your integration contact.

## POST `/bet_report/list`

`report_unit`: `min` or `day`. `time_unit`: `bet` or `settle`. `perpage` 1–1000.

## POST `/jp_rec/list`, `/jp_rec/detail`

Same shape as bet list/detail.

## POST `/scratch_card_rec/list`

Optional `status`: `PENDING`, `CLAIMED`, `SCRATCHED`. Optional `lang`.
