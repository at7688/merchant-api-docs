Read-only. Unix **seconds**. `end_at` must be greater than `start_at`.

These four are limited to **10** calls per merchant per minute: `/bet_rec/list`, `/jp_rec/list`, `/bet_report/list`, `/scratch_card_rec/list`.

`acc` is the player id without prefix; omit for all players.

## POST `/game/list`

Inner `{}` is fine. Each item: `code` (use as `game_code`), `name`, `game_category_code`, `game_type_code`, `id`.

## POST `/bet_rec/list`

`page` ≥ 1, `perpage` 1–1000, `start_at`, `end_at`, optional `acc`. Success: `list`, `row_count`, `page_count`.

## POST `/bet_rec/detail`

`{ "sn": "...", "lang": "en" }`. `lang` is required (BCP 47).

## POST `/bet_rec/query`

Up to 100 sns. Response items: `sn`, `status`. `UNKNOWN` means we have no row — **do not auto-refund**. Contact ops with the sn.

## POST `/bet_report/list`

`report_unit`: `min` or `day`. `time_unit`: `bet` or `settle`.

## POST `/jp_rec/list`, `/jp_rec/detail`

Same shape as bet list / detail.

## POST `/scratch_card_rec/list`

Optional `status`: `PENDING`, `CLAIMED`, `SCRATCHED`.
