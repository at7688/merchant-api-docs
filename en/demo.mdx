title: Demo play
description: The demo link, its parameters and its limits. No integration required.
---

Demo play does not go through the merchant API. Open the link and you are in — no credentials, no player to create. Use it to evaluate the games before integrating.

## Links

| Environment | Link |
|---|---|
| Test | `https://game-lobby.at-dev.app/demo` |
| Production | `https://game-lobby.at-gaming.app/demo` |

## Parameters

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| `currency` | Demo currency. Unset = `TWD`. Only a currency already used by an agent on the platform is accepted; anything else falls back to an existing platform currency |
| `game` | Game code, case-insensitive. When set, the lobby is skipped and that game opens directly. Take it from `code` in `/game/list` |

`https://game-lobby.at-gaming.app/demo?currency=TWD&game=HSZN`

## Accounts

Opening the link borrows an account from the platform's demo pool. No registration.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Points | Reset to 1,000,000 on every borrow |
| Hold | Not lent to anyone else for 2 hours from the borrow |
| Per source address | 10 per hour; beyond that entry is refused |

## Constraints {#constraints}

1. Demo accounts belong to the platform's demo agent, not to any merchant. Demo bets and accounting never appear in a merchant's `/bet_rec/*` or `/bet_report/*`.
2. Demo play does not take part in JP.
3. Demo points are not funds. Neither `/player/*` nor `/transfer/*` applies to a demo account.
