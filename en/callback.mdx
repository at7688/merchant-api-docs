Seamless wallet only. Platform `POST`s to `{MerchantApiUrl}{path}` (e.g. `https://merchant.example/player/bet`). Base must not end with `/`. Body `{ "data": "<hex>" }` with no `agent_acc`. Same ApiKey. [Protocol](/en/protocol). Timeout 15 seconds. Response JSON must include top-level `code`. bet/win `data.original_balance` and `data.balance` are numbers.

<Warning>
Success: `code` is `OK`.
</Warning>

Timeout 15 seconds. The same packet is not retried immediately. The **same `sn`** may later receive another `/player/win` or `/player/refund` (settle retry, operator resend, stuck-bet refund). One exception: a failed **tournament** payout is **never retried automatically and cannot be resent by an operator** — it is handled outside the system. Granted-spin consumption does **not** send `/player/bet`; settle may still send `/player/win` (`amount` may be 0). `acc` on bet/win/refund is unprefixed; only the balance callback sends both prefixed `acc` and `player_acc`. Idempotency on `sn`. `/player/win` may arrive after disconnect.

<Warning>
Keep `sn` de-duplication records for **at least 90 days**; do not use a short TTL. Operator resends have no deadline — a `win` or `refund` that timed out days or weeks ago may be resent today, and an expired de-duplication record means the merchant credits it twice. The platform's own bet lifecycle is kept for 60 days.
</Warning>

## POST `{base}/player/win`

Credit. `game_code` comes from three sources — **not every payout maps to `/game/list`**:

| `game_code` | `sn` shape | Preceding `/player/bet` | Reconcile with |
|---|---|---|---|
| a `/game/list` `code` | bet id | yes | `/bet_rec/query` |
| [`TOURNAMENT`](/en/enums#game-code) | `TOUR_{tournament}_{rank}_{member}` | no | no query endpoint; use your own ledger |
| [`SCRATCH_CARD`](/en/enums#game-code) | `SC_{ticket}` | no | [`/scratch_card_rec/list`](/en/records) |

<Warning>
Do not reject `TOURNAMENT` or `SCRATCH_CARD` payouts because their `game_code` is absent from
`/game/list`, and do not treat them as invalid because `/bet_rec/query` returns
`BET_NOT_FOUND` — neither one is a bet.
</Warning>

<Warning>
`refund` only returns what the `/player/bet` with the same `sn` actually debited. If the merchant never received that bet, or rejected it with an error `code`, it must return `OK` without crediting. The platform sends `refund` as soon as a bet's outcome is unknown, so the bet may never have reached the merchant.
</Warning>

### Retries and deadline

Until it receives `OK`, the platform retries the **same `sn`**: first after 60 seconds, then
exponential backoff (60 × 2^n seconds) capped at 1 hour per interval, with a **24-hour total
deadline**. A single refund may be retried a dozen or more times within that window — size your
de-duplication records and alert thresholds accordingly.

After 24 hours the platform **stops retrying**. The refund moves to "overdue" and an operator
resends it or closes it by hand; that happens at an unpredictable time, and until it does the
two ledgers stay out of balance. Return `OK` within 24 hours.
