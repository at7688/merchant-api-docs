---
title: Players
description: Create, balance (transfer wallet), login, logout. Do not send the agent prefix in acc.
---

Inner `acc` is the player account only (alphanum 6–60). Stored as `{agent_acc}_{acc}`.

## POST `/player/add`

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `acc` | yes | alphanum 6–60 |
| `name` | yes | max 30 |

Success `data`: `{ "acc": "kiwi01_jordan", "player_acc": "jordan", "player_id": 123 }`.

Error: `ACC_EXIST`.

## POST `/player/balance`

**Transfer wallet only.** Seamless merchants implement `/player/balance` on their side.

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| `acc` | yes |

Success `data`: `currency`, `balance`.

## POST `/player/login`

Returns a lobby or game URL.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `acc` | yes | alphanum 6–60 |
| `lang` | yes | BCP 47, e.g. `zh-TW` |
| `ip` | yes | Player IP |
| `device` | yes | `DESKTOP` or `MOBILE` |
| `game_code` | no | Omit to open the lobby |
| `return_url` | no | Valid URL |

Success `data`: `{ "url": "https://..." }`.

Errors: `ACC_NOT_FOUND`, `ACC_NOT_ACTIVE`, `ACC_SUSPEND`, `GAME_NOT_FOUND`, `GAME_MAINTAIN`.

## POST `/player/logout`

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `acc` | no | Omit to log out all members under the merchant |
